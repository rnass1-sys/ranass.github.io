INPUT:
- Hardcoded array of 10 last names (last_names)
- Hardcoded parallel array of 10 exam scores (exam_scores)

PROCESSING:
- Initialize last_names and exam_scores arrays with 10 corresponding elements
- Call display_data(last_names, exam_scores):
    - Loop through index i from 0 to length of array - 1
    - Print last_names[i] and exam_scores[i]
- Call display_reverse(last_names, exam_scores):
    - Loop through index i from length of array - 1 down to 0
    - Print last_names[i] and exam_scores[i]

OUTPUT:
- All 10 student last names paired with their exam scores in forward order
- All 10 student last names paired with their exam scores in reverse order
