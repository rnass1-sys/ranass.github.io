INPUT:
  - tickets (int)

PROCESSING:
  - Get number of tickets from user.
  - Establish volume threshold evaluation checks:
      - tickets >= 25       -> price = $50
      - tickets 10 to 24    -> price = $60
      - tickets 5 to 9      -> price = $70
      - tickets less than 5 -> price = $75
  - Calculate total cost = tickets * price per ticket

OUTPUT:
  - tickets (int)
  - price_per_ticket (float) [Decimals aligned]
  - total_cost (float)       [Decimals aligned]
