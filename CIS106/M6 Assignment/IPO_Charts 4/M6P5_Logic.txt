INPUT:
  - last_name (str)
  - salary (float)
  - job_level (int)

PROCESSING:
  - Get employee last name, base salary, and job rank level.
  - Match performance job level tiers against rate matrix maps:
      - level >= 10 -> bonus rate = 25%
      - level 5-9   -> bonus rate = 20%
      - All others  -> bonus rate = 10%
  - Calculate bonus amount = salary * bonus rate

OUTPUT:
  - last_name (str)
  - bonus_amount (float) [Decimals aligned]
