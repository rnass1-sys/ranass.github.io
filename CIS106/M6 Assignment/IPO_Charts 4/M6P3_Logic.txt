INPUT:
  - principal (float)
  - years (int)

PROCESSING:
  - Get principal CD balance and maturity timeline from user.
  - Determine compound interest structure using relational logic arrays:
      - principal > $100,000 AND 5 years               -> rate = 6%
      - principal between $50k-$100k AND 10 years     -> rate = 5%
      - principal between $50k-$100k AND 5 years      -> rate = 4%
      - Unmatched fallback criteria                   -> rate = 2%
  - Calculate first year interest = principal * rate

OUTPUT:
  - principal (float)           [Decimals aligned]
  - interest_rate (float)       [Decimals aligned]
  - first_year_interest (float) [Decimals aligned]
