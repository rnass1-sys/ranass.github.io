INPUT:
  - Sequential read lines from text file:
    - item (string)
    - quantity (int)
    - price (float)

PROCESSING:
  1. Step through the file record blocks line-by-line.
  2. Compute line execution pricing: extended_price = quantity * price.
  3. Accumulate line cost into total_extended_price.
  4. Count the loop transaction cycle into order_count.
  5. Post-loop, compute mean value: average_order = total_extended_price / order_count.

OUTPUT:
  - Columnar report mapping Item, Qty, Price, and Extended Price.
  - Final metrics: Sum of all prices, total orders count, and average order price.
